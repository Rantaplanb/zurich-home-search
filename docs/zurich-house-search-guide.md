# Zurich Home Search Guide

## Market Reality

As of June 1, 2025, the city of Zurich reported only 235 vacant apartments, which corresponds to a vacancy rate of 0.1%. That is effectively a seller's market for rentals. At the same time, the city reports roughly 2,300 apartment tenant changes per month, with higher turnover around April and October. The practical takeaway is:

- Do not wait for the market to become easier.
- Search aggressively across multiple channels.
- Apply very quickly once you see a strong option.
- Use your first month in Zurich to attend viewings in person and submit same-day applications.

## Best Search Strategy

### 1. Search by commute, not just by postcode

Your office is at `Beethovenstrasse 48`, so optimize around practical travel time instead of insisting on a small set of central districts. A flat that is 20 to 30 minutes away by reliable tram or S-Bahn can be much better value than a more expensive flat in a prestige area.

Start by evaluating options in three commute bands:

- `0 to 15 minutes`
- `15 to 25 minutes`
- `25 to 35 minutes`

Nearby municipalities can also be worth considering if they have direct rail or tram connections.

### 2. Run three search tracks in parallel

Do not search only for one final perfect apartment. Run all of these at the same time:

- `Private apartment search`
- `WG / room / sublet search`
- `Co-op / non-profit housing search`

This gives you a better chance of landing something good quickly while keeping optionality.

### 3. Treat a good interim place as a strategic win

Since you can stay with a friend for a month or more, you are in a stronger position than many new arrivals. Use that flexibility to avoid panic-signing a bad lease. A strong short-term WG or sublet can be a very good bridge if the ideal long-term flat does not appear immediately.

## How To Improve Your Odds

### Keep a ready-to-send dossier

Have a single clean PDF package ready with:

- passport or ID copy
- signed work contract
- recent payslips if available
- short note explaining that you are relocating from Greece
- employer or HR contact for reference
- proof of savings if useful

If you do not yet have a Swiss `Betreibungsregisterauszug`, say that clearly and explain that you are newly relocating to Switzerland.

### Apply on the same day

In Zurich, speed matters. If you attend a viewing and the place is a serious option, submit the application that day.

### Use a short German intro

Even if your full dossier is in English, a short, correct German message helps. This is especially useful for WG applications, where personality fit matters more.

### Stay comfortably under your maximum budget

Landlords often assess affordability conservatively. If the gross monthly rent looks clearly manageable relative to your salary, your file is stronger.

## Good Search Discipline

### Set alerts everywhere

Set notifications on all major portals before arrival. The goal is to see promising listings as close to posting time as possible.

### Track options in one sheet

For every listing, record:

- monthly base rent
- total monthly cost
- deposit
- size
- address
- commute to `Beethovenstrasse 48`
- nearest supermarket
- nearest gym with sauna
- viewing date
- application deadline
- subjective notes

### Widen the radius before increasing the budget

If response rates are weak after 10 to 14 days, widen the search area first. In many cases, that improves value much more than simply paying more.

## District and Area Notes

The city rent survey shows meaningful differences between quarters. Cheaper quarters include `Schwamendingen`, `Affoltern`, and `Leimbach`, while more expensive areas include `Kreis 1`, `Riesbach`, and `Hottingen`.

That does not mean you should avoid expensive quarters entirely. It means you should be deliberate about whether the premium actually buys something you value, such as commute time, lake access, quieter streets, or neighborhood atmosphere.

## What To Check At A Viewing

- street noise during the day and at night
- natural light
- storage
- laundry situation
- internet options
- building condition
- kitchen and bathroom quality
- heating and insulation
- floor level and elevator
- privacy
- distance to tram, train, and supermarket
- realistic door-to-desk commute
- gym options nearby, especially sauna access

## Scam Avoidance

- Never transfer money before an in-person viewing and a signed contract.
- Be suspicious if the owner claims to be abroad and asks for money to ship keys.
- Treat unusually cheap listings with caution.
- Check whether a sublet is properly authorized if you go for a room or temporary flat.

## Suggested 30-Day Plan

### Before arrival

- Create alerts on all major portals.
- Prepare your PDF dossier.
- Prepare a short English and German intro.
- Define your real budget ceiling and your preferred commute bands.

### Week 1

- Test actual commute routes to `Beethovenstrasse 48`.
- Attend as many viewings as possible.
- Start with a wide radius.

### Week 2

- Tighten your shortlist based on commute, value, and neighborhood fit.
- Apply same day for strong options.
- Keep at least one pragmatic fallback track active.

### Weeks 3 and 4

- Rebalance if needed: widen geography, adjust minimum size, or switch emphasis between solo flat and WG.
- Do not let a romantic idea of the perfect neighborhood block you from taking a strong overall option.

## Working Rule For Your Search

The right Zurich apartment is usually not the prettiest listing. It is the one that gives the best combined result across:

- all-in monthly cost
- value for money
- reliable commute
- neighborhood fit
- daily convenience
- realistic acceptance probability

The scoring system in `house_scoring.py` is designed to help with that.
